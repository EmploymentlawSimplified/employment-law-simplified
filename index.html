Who This Is For

This Site Exists Because the System Isn't Fair

Most employers have lawyers. Most employees don't. That power imbalance means thousands of people every year either give up valid claims or walk into hearings unprepared.

Employment Law Simplified is built to change that. Every guide, every template recommendation, every piece of strategic advice on this site is written from the employee's perspective —[...]

This isn't legal advice. It's something many people find just as valuable: a clear, strategic understanding of how the system works and how to use it effectively.
