About

Why This Site Exists

Employment Law Simplified — strategic guidance from the employee's side of the table.

When you're facing a workplace dispute, the first thing you discover is that the system isn't designed to help you. Your employer has HR departments, legal teams, and procedures built to protect the company. You have a search engine and a growing sense that you're on your own.

I know that feeling because I've lived it.

I went through the employment tribunal process as a litigant in person — representing myself, without a solicitor, against a large corporate employer with a full legal team behind them. I learned how grievance processes actually work (and how they're often used against you). I learned how employers build narratives, manipulate timelines, and use procedural complexity as a weapon. And I learned that the law itself, once you strip away the jargon, is far more accessible than the legal profession wants you to believe.

That experience is why Employment Law Simplified exists.

What This Site Is

This is a strategic resource for UK employees navigating workplace disputes. Every guide on this site is written from the employee's perspective — not the employer's, not a solicitor's. The goal is simple: give you the knowledge and strategic understanding to make informed decisions about your situation and, if necessary, to represent yourself effectively.

We cover employment tribunals, discrimination claims, grievances, unfair dismissal, evidence gathering, and the practical realities of being a litigant in person. We also review and recommend the books, templates, and tools that genuinely help — because having the right resources makes a material difference to your case.

What This Site Is Not

This site does not provide legal advice. We provide information, strategic guidance, and practical resources based on real experience and thorough research. Every situation is different, and for advice specific to your circumstances, you should consult a qualified employment solicitor or seek help from ACAS, Citizens Advice, or your trade union.

What we do offer is something many people find just as valuable: a clear, honest explanation of how the system works, written by someone who has been on your side of the table.

The Principle

Employment law doesn't have to be complicated. The system works better when employees understand their rights — and that understanding shouldn't cost hundreds of pounds an hour.

If you're going through a workplace dispute, you're not alone. Start with our complete tribunal guide, or browse our latest articles to find guidance relevant to your situation.
