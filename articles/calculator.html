How the figures are worked out

​

1. Basic award

The basic award works like statutory redundancy pay. For each full year of service (up to 20 years) you receive:

  • 1.5 weeks' pay for each year you were aged 41 or over;
  • 1 week's pay for each year you were aged 22 to 40;
  • 0.5 week's pay for each year you were under 22.

A week's pay is capped at £719 (2025/26), so the maximum basic award is £21,570.

​

2. Compensatory award

This reflects your actual financial loss — mainly lost earnings from the date your job ended until you find comparable work, based on your net weekly pay. It is capped at the lower of £118,223 or 52 weeks' gross pay. You have a duty to mitigate your loss by looking for new work.

​

3. Loss of statutory rights

A small award (commonly around £500) to reflect that you have lost accrued employment rights, such as having to build up two years' service again before you can bring another unfair dismissal claim.

​

4. Injury to feelings (discrimination only)

If your claim includes discrimination, tribunals can award compensation for injury to feelings using the Vento bands. These are updated each year — the figures above are approximate mid-points. This does not apply to a straightforward unfair dismissal claim.
