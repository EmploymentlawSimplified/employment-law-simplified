Free: Official Tribunal Forms

The official government forms, straight from GOV.UK, published under the Open Government Licence. These are free, and we link directly to the government's own page so you always get the current version. Bookmark this page — you'll likely need to come back to it more than once.

ET1 — Make a Claim to an Employment Tribunal

The form you use to start a tribunal claim on your own. Use our ET1 Drafting Guide below for help completing it well.

Download ET1 from GOV.UK →

Free — official government form

ET1A — Make a Claim With Others

Use this alongside the ET1 if you're bringing a claim together with one or more other claimants against the same employer.

Download ET1A from GOV.UK →

Free — official government form

ET3 — Employer's Response Form

The response your employer (the Respondent) must submit within 28 days of your claim being served, via the official online service. Worth reading so you know what to expect back.

View the ET3 response service on GOV.UK →
