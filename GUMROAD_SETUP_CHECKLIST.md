**Recommended price: £6.99** · **Slug: `grievance-letter`**

**Description:**
A structured, ready-to-adapt letter for raising a formal grievance against your employer, with guidance notes explaining what to include, what to leave out, and why each section matters. Raising a grievance properly — in writing, clearly, and calmly — can be a protected act and shows a tribunal you acted reasonably if your case goes further.

This template walks you through setting out the facts, the specific policy or right you say was breached, the outcome you're asking for, and how to keep the tone factual rather than emotional, which matters both to getting a fair hearing internally and to how the letter reads if it's ever seen by a tribunal.

This is a template and guidance, not legal advice — adapt the wording to your own situation and get advice on anything you're unsure about.

Includes a companion "How to Use" guide with a step-by-step walkthrough, where to find each piece of information, and common mistakes to avoid. Instant digital download (2 Word .docx files). Pairs with our Complete Process & Time Limits Guide.

### Product 3: Subject Access Request (SAR) Template
**Recommended price: £6.99** · **Slug: `sar`**

**Description:**
A ready-to-adapt letter for requesting a copy of your own personal data from your employer under UK GDPR — including internal notes, emails, and correspondence that may not otherwise be shared with you. This is often the single most useful step for uncovering what's actually in your file, especially if you suspect records have been altered, backdated, or kept from you.

The template covers what to ask for, how to phrase the request so it can't easily be narrowed by your employer, and what to do if the one-month statutory deadline is missed.

This is a template and guidance, not legal advice.

Includes a companion "How to Use" guide covering when to send it, how to make it stronger, and where to escalate if it's ignored. Instant digital download (2 Word .docx files). Pairs with our Complete Process & Time Limits Guide and our article on evidence and record manipulation.

- Ready-to-adapt UK GDPR data request letter, plus a full companion guide

- Notes on the one-month response deadline and what to do if missed

**Description:**
