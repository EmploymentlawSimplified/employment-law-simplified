## 1. Note on scope vs. the website/Gumroad edition

This e-book is a **reading edition** — the full roadmap, time limits, ACAS process, 23-chapter walkthrough, current Vento bands, tax treatment, evidence checklist, and glossary. It does **not** include the fillable Word-style case tracker or key-contacts table from the Gumroad `.docx` version — those don't work in an e-reader. This is intentional and works in your favour: the e-book is a lower-priced, wider-reach front door, and it actively promotes the fillable Gumroad edition and the other templates in its back matter, so readers who want the tracker have somewhere to go.

## 4. Upload content

- [ ] **Cover:** upload `kdp-ebook-cover.jpg` (already at the exact 1600×2560px spec — no need to use KDP's Cover Creator)
- [ ] Use KDP's online previewer to check the table of contents navigates correctly and no pages look broken — check at minimum: the cover, title page, disclaimer page, one chapter, and the table with the Vento bands (tables can occasionally reflow oddly on small e-reader screens — worth a specific look)
- [ ] **DRM:** your choice — most independent publishers in this space leave DRM off, since buyers of self-help/legal-guide content aren't a piracy risk group and DRM-free is more convenient for a stressed reader who wants to read across devices

## 5. Pricing tab

- [ ] Select **70% royalty** — requires a list price between $2.99–$9.99 (or local-currency equivalent) and enrollment in pricing across all Amazon marketplaces
- [ ] **Recommended UK list price: £3.99** (sits at roughly the midpoint of the 70%-royalty band, well under the £19.99 Gumroad price, so it acts as a discovery/lead product rather than competing with it)
- [ ] Territories: **All territories** (required for the 70% royalty tier anyway)
- [ ] **KDP Select:** optional — enrolling makes the book exclusive to Amazon for 90 days but unlocks Kindle Unlimited page-read royalties and promotional tools (free days, countdown deals). Since your main revenue is the website/Gumroad ecosystem, exclusivity costs you little; KU page-reads can add a small extra income stream. Worth turning on unless you specifically want the EPUB available elsewhere too.
